# ShieldDrive Insurance — Internal Pricing Memo (FICTIONAL)

> This document is fictional and exists only to demonstrate the Decision Impact
> Simulator. ShieldDrive Insurance is not a real company.

**Date:** 2026-10-28
**From:** Pricing & Actuarial team
**To:** Executive committee
**Subject:** Proposed 12% motor premium increase at renewal

## Background

Motor claims costs rose 17% over the last 12 months, driven mainly by higher
repair costs (parts and labour up ~14%) and a 9% increase in average bodily-injury
settlement values. Reinsurance renewed in July 2026 at a 21% higher rate. Our
combined operating ratio has moved from 97% to 104%, meaning the motor book is now
loss-making before investment income.

## Proposed action

Apply a **12% increase to all renewing motor policies** from 1 January 2027. The
rise applies at renewal only, not mid-term. No change to coverage or excess levels.

## Expected impact (internal estimate)

- Approx. 1.2 million renewing policies affected.
- Average annual premium rises from £640 to £717.
- Modelled additional churn of 4–7 percentage points above the 11% baseline.
- Net revenue uplift estimated at £78m–£96m if churn stays within range.

## Retention mitigations under discussion

- Loyalty credit of up to £40 for customers with 5+ continuous years.
- Optional higher-excess tier to let price-sensitive drivers reduce premiums.
- Proactive outbound calls to the top 50,000 at-risk renewals.

## Known risks

- Consumer advocacy groups may frame the rise as "loyalty penalty" pricing.
- The regulator has an open thematic review into fair value and price walking.
- Competitor repricing is uncertain; a slower competitor rise would worsen churn.
